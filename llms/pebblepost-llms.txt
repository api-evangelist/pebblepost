# PebblePost


> Drive incremental revenue with Performance CTV and Programmatic Direct Mail. Built for marketers, backed by real transactions.


## About PebblePost
PebblePost is a commerce marketing platform that turns billions of identity, intent, and transaction data signals into incremental revenue for leading brands. It enables marketers to target decision-ready consumers, defend every dollar, and know with certainty what drives growth across Performance CTV and Programmatic Direct Mail.


## Core Solutions


- **Programmatic Direct Mail (PDM)**: Physical marketing sent only to households most likely to convert, bridging offline and online strategies, increasing ROI, and reducing waste.
- **Performance CTV**: Connected TV advertising campaigns built for measurable performance, moving beyond awareness to optimize conversions and incremental business impact.


## Use Cases


- **Acquisition Solutions**: Designed to Identify, engage, and convert new customers with precision.
- **Retention Solutions**: Designed to deepen relationships, upsell, cross-sell, and win back lapsed customers.


## Technology & Data


- **Performance Marketing Engine**: Automates targeting, optimization, and measurement for speed, scale, and accuracy.
- **PebblePost Graph**: Securely connects billions of first-party household, online intent, and transaction data points within a privacy-by-design framework to fuel reach and relevance.
- **PebblePost Platform**: A single, unified platform that makes it easy to onboard data, define marketing goals, and measure success without manual work.


## Sustainability
PebblePost focuses on sustainable direct mail by targeting only high-intent consumers, partnering with environmental restoration platforms like Veritree, and offering FSC® Certified paper options.
